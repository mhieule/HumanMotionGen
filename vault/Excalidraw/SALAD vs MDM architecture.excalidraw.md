What SALAD changes compared with MDM ^79ncpv34

1 ^UguphDko

Two stages: a VAE is trained first and frozen; diffusion then runs in its latent space instead of on raw 263-D poses. ^D4XjZeMx

2 ^indSIi1q

Body-structured input: the 263-D vector is split back into 22 joints, each with its own MLP (MDM: one Linear over all 263 numbers). ^jq0ukEt3

3 ^AqooyzkZ

Skeleto-temporal convolutions + pooling along the bone graph (22 → 12 → 7 parts) and time (N → N/4). ^8aYviaZG

4 ^Z3TVkiPM

Latent = grid of body parts × time (N/4 × 7 × 32 ≈ 5× fewer numbers than N × 263); each number belongs to one part at one moment. ^meTzSdYu

5 ^J1w58ewp

Word-level text: cross-attention to every CLIP token, so words can attach to specific parts and times (enables attention-based editing). ^J3sgTBbX

6 ^bLJGRg1S

Diffusion step enters through FiLM in every sub-block (MDM: one extra token in the sequence). ^hEFrhziK

7 ^8dwyZS0I

Factorised attention: temporal, then skeletal, then cross-attention, instead of one self-attention over all frames. ^0dG75pAN

8 ^Y22cNqQq

v-prediction + DDIM (50 steps) instead of x₀-prediction + DDPM. ^pGA6rsOh

Same as MDM: frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 · 10% text dropout → classifier-free guidance
transformer backbone · 1000 training noise steps · HumanML3D 263-D in and out · same Guo et al. evaluator ^TrCRnxIw

Paper FID: SALAD 0.076 vs MDM-50 0.489 · our RTX 3060: training ≈ 3 days for both
(SALAD: VAE ~42 h + denoiser ~29 h) · our demo: SALAD slides its feet most (14.7 cm/s) ^1oIEr9sV
